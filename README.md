## Jack Manners

Sleep researcher at [Flinders University](https://researchnow.flinders.edu.au/en/persons/jack-manners), Adelaide, and a big geek on the side. I build tools and models that improve sleep health through sharper diagnosis, more targeted treatment, better management and smarter risk assessment.

Always happy to talk sleep, wearables or data pipelines.

---

### Research interests

| Area | What I'm after |
|---|---|
| **Obstructive sleep apnoea** | Endotyping, and how night-to-night variability changes what we think we know about severity and risk |
| **Consumer sleep technology** | Under-mattress sensors, rings, wearables and apps, and whether they can be trusted for clinical care |
| **Stats and algorithms** | Methods for large, messy, real-world sleep data: millions of nights, many devices, plenty of missing values |
| **Digital health trials** | Evidence for accessible diagnosis, including [ACCESS-OSA](https://doi.org/10.1093/sleepadvances/zpag023), a multi-site RCT of OSA screening |

---

### Technical work

| Capability | In practice |
|---|---|
| **Device data at scale** | Integrations with consumer device APIs (Withings, Fitbit and others) feeding research pipelines |
| **Trial infrastructure** | REDCap tooling for recruitment and allocation reporting, offline capture and multi-site operations |
| **Signal processing** | Pipelines for EDF/PSG files and EEG, from raw recordings to topographic and spectral outputs |
| **Modelling** | Large-scale sleep and OSA modelling, vigilance and shift-work performance, psychometrics and factor analysis |
| **Data stewardship** | Custodian of a repository spanning 20+ studies and 1,000+ participants, including the Raine Study |
